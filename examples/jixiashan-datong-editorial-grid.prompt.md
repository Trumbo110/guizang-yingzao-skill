# 既下山·大同测试提示词

- 模式：`editorial-grid`
- 用例：`style-transfer`
- 输入角色：用户照片是 edit target，也是建筑几何的唯一来源。
- 输出：3:4 竖版建筑旅行海报。

## 提示词

```text
Transform the supplied real photograph of 既下山·大同酒店 in 大同古城东南邑 into a refined contemporary Chinese architecture editorial poster in the editorial-grid mode. The hotel is a contemporary courtyard project completed in 2025, not an ancient monument.

Keep the exact photographed courtyard, central axis, stepped passage, reflecting pool, paired stone figures, trees, roof slopes, gray walls, camera viewpoint, proportions, openings, lighting logic, and strong bilateral symmetry recognizable. Preserve the water reflection as a central compositional anchor. Do not add, remove, redesign, rebuild, or fantasize any building, roof, floor, doorway, plaque, ornament, statue, tree, or water feature.

Retain a recognizable edited photograph as the base. Simplify it into 3–4 major tonal bands, cool charcoal and blue-gray architecture on a warm off-white paper field, with one restrained modern brick-red accent (#AC3C33). Add selective fine architectural linework only on roof edges, courtyard axes, and the water boundary. Add subtle halftone and tactile offset-print paper grain, never enough to obscure geometry. Contemporary architectural magazine tone; calm, precise, and cultured, not nostalgic fantasy.

Use a strict 6-column editorial grid with 5–8% margins. Let the architecture bleed toward the bottom and side edges while keeping the axial pool visible. Use the open sky/top area for clean typography and measured negative space. One brick-red rectangle or narrow rule may organize the title. Clear three-level hierarchy, no decorative icons, no fake stamp, no QR code.

Render exactly and only: "既下山 · 大同", "东南邑", "SUNYATA DATONG", "2025". Use a refined modern Song/Ming editorial face for the main Chinese title, vertical type for "东南邑", and restrained modern sans serif for English and year. All text appears exactly once.

Preserve the real architecture and photographic identity. No fake antiquity, no Japanese motifs, no lanterns, no dragons, no clouds, no extra slogans, no invented microtext, no logos, no watermark.
```

- 项目地点为山西大同，竣工时间为 2025 年，建筑设计为 goa 大象设计。
- 项目位于大同古城东南邑历史文化街区；新建主体被设计方描述为吸收山西民居传统的当代院落，而不是仿古复刻。
